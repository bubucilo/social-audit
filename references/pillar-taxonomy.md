# Starter category taxonomy

Classify into 7–10 fine-grained categories first, then merge into ≤5 pillars in the
playbook. Rename them in the brand's own words and language.

| Category | What goes in | Typical job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How-to / guide | Step-by-step, rules, checklists, "how to" | Saves, authority |
| Emotional / aspiration | POV, longing, family moments, prayers/wishes, reply-to-comment videos, "who wants to…" | Comments, reach |
| Practical tips | Packing, logistics, where to eat/go, documents, transport | Shares |
| Product proof | Venue/product review, side-by-side tests, unboxing | Consideration |
| News / regulation | Rule changes, official announcements, industry news | Shares |
| Values / wisdom | Quotes, scripture, lessons, brand philosophy | Brand depth |
| Story / history | Heritage, origin stories, "then vs now" | Saves, steady reach |
| Journal / BTS | Behind-the-scenes, trip or event documentation, recaps | Warm-audience trust |
| Testimonial | Customer voice, reviews, case stories | Conversion |
| Promo / brand | Packages, prices, launches, hiring, partnerships | Direct response |

## Rule-writing tips
- Order rules most-specific first: testimonial → product → promo → journal → how-to →
  values → default. Named-person patterns (e.g. `(Mrs|Mr|Ibu|Bapak) [A-Z]`) catch
  testimonials early.
- Pick the default as the category that is most often "none of the above" (usually the
  emotional/aspiration bucket), then fix misfiles with `overrides`. Budget for 15–30
  overrides per 300 posts.
- Cross-posted captions: use `--inherit` so both platforms agree.
- Split a bucket when it hides a pattern. In the test account, "values" and "history"
  behaved differently (short video vs slideshow), so they became two categories.

## Merging into ≤5 pillars (playbook step)
Merge by the job the content does, not by topic. For example:
- How-to → an educational pillar (saves)
- Emotional / aspiration → a community pillar (comments, leads)
- Practical tips + Product proof + News → a readiness pillar (shares)
- Values + Story → an authority pillar (brand depth)
- Testimonial + Journal + Promo → a proof pillar (conversion)

A category with a 0–5% hit rate and a large share of posts (in the test account,
journal + testimonial were 33% of posts) becomes the smallest pillar or moves to
Stories. Say so explicitly.
