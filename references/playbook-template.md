# Playbook template (`playbook.md`)

One playbook per brand per run. It covers every audited platform, with ≤5 pillars.
Hooks and example lines are in the brand's content language, with its spelling and
form of address. Structure and explanations follow the user's language.

```markdown
# <Brand> Content Pillar Playbook: <Platforms>

<date> · <owner>

<Lead: N pillars, built from the <date> audits of X <platform> posts and Y <platform> posts.>

## The <N> pillars at a glance
<One sentence on which pillars carry the weight and why (proven organic winners).>
| Pillar | Job | Main KPI | Proof from the audit | <Platform A> share | <Platform B> share |

Volume: <posts/week per platform>. <What moves to Stories.>

## Pillar 1 — <Name> (<source categories>)
**Objective:** <what this pillar does for the business, one or two sentences>. Target: <KPI number vs baseline>.

**Hook formulas**
1. <Formula name>: "<line in content language>" (taken from a real winner)
2. ...

**Format**
| | <Platform A> | <Platform B> |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Hero / Length / Footage / Close ...> | ... | ... |

**Example content**
| # | Title / opening line | Format | Beats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1..6 | ... | ... | ... |

<Guardrail line if this pillar has a trap (for example guilt hooks).>

... Pillars 2–5 the same way ...

## Weekly rhythm
| Day | <Platform A> | <Platform B> |
- Cross-post rule, reply/comment routine, news turnaround.

## Guardrails
- [ ] <brand-voice checks: tone, spelling, banned words, CTA, emoji, captions length from the audit>
**Stop posting to the feed**
- <categories/formats with near-zero hit rate, with the number>

## Measurement
Review on <date = run date + 30 days> against organic baselines.
| Metric | <A> baseline | <A> target | <B> baseline | <B> target |
- Hit = 2× median; track hit rate per pillar monthly.
- Open question(s) for the owner (paid confirmation).

Data: `<platform>-audit.md`, `stats-*.md`, `data/` in this folder.
```

Checks before saving:
- Every pillar's proof number exists in a stats file, and none is a paid-flagged post.
- No example breaks the brand guardrails.
- There are ≤5 pillars, and each has exactly one primary KPI.
